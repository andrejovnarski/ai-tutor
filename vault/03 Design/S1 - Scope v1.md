# Section 1: Scope of v1

**One strand: number sense → addition and subtraction.** About 25–35 small skills.

| Grade | Skills |
|---|---|
| K | Count to 20, compare numbers, add and take away within 10 |
| 1 | Add and subtract within 20, place value (tens and ones) |
| 2 | Add and subtract within 100, word problems |

Fits both age groups: a 5-year-old starts at counting; an older kid with gaps lands on the missing skill.

See the draft graph: [[Skill Map]].

## Not in v1
- Multiply/divide, fractions, geometry, time, money
- Mood sensing, interest themes
- Parent dashboard and controls
- Native app, other languages

## Why this strand
It is the base of all later math, it serves both age groups, and its skills form a clear chain — so adaptation is easy to show.
