# Product Vision

An AI math tutor for kids aged 5–11 that adapts in real time, on two axes:

1. **Level** — too easy → harder; stuck → step back and fill the missing earlier skill.
2. **Teaching style** — if a kid is stuck, explain another way (number line, blocks, story, drawing) and remember what works for that kid.

Later (not v1): mood sensing, interest themes (e.g. dinosaurs).

## Who pays
Families first. Schools and investors later, as a learning platform. See [[Users]].

## Core rule
**AI never grades math.** Code checks answers and tracks skills. AI only talks and teaches. See [[Decision - Approach]].
