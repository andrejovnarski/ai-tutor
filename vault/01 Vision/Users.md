# Users

## Kid (5–11)
- **5–7:** early readers. Need voice in and out, big touch targets, pictures.
- **8–11:** can read and type some. May have gaps in earlier skills.

## Parent
- Signs up, gives consent (COPPA — kid is under 13), adds kid profile(s).
- Gets a **weekly report**: sessions done, skills learned, where stuck.
- Pays (later). Not in v1: live dashboard, goals, time limits.

## Later
- Schools, as a learning platform.
- Investors (need proof of learning; see [[Success Metrics]]).
